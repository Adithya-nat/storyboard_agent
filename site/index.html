Questions

      

        
What does a film cost?
You pay Google directly through your own key. A 30-second film with on-camera dialogue has cost us about $5 to $8; most of that is AI video. The studio shows an estimate before you start and the real cost after.

        
How long does a film take?
Usually 10 to 20 minutes for 30 seconds. You can watch each step live and leave the page; the film keeps rendering.

        
Which languages can I make films in?
English and Tamil. A film is entirely in one language, from the dialogue to the captions and the post copy.

        
Is my key safe?
Your key is stored only in your browser. It travels with the request that starts or resumes a film, is held in memory while that film renders, and is never written to the database or logs. You can remove it any time.

        
Who can sign in?
Anyone can sign in with GitHub or Google. During the beta, new accounts are let in in small batches, so you may wait a little before you can make films.

        
Can I run it without the hosted beta?
Yes. Clone both repositories, add your key to .env and run make setup && make dev. Nothing leaves your machine except calls to Google.
