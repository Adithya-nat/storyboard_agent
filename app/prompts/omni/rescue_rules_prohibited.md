The block is about PROHIBITED content, usually violence, weapons, injury, restraint, crime shown in progress, or danger to people.
- Show the moment indirectly: the aftermath, a reaction shot, a calm composition or a cutaway instead of the act itself.
- Remove weapons, blood, injuries, restraints (handcuffs, ropes), people lying hurt and anyone being overpowered from the frame. Officials can be present and in control without force.
- Soften dialogue that describes harm in detail; keep the plot information.
